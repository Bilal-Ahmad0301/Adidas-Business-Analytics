# 📊 Adidas Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an interactive sales analytics dashboard built using Microsoft Power BI, based on the publicly available Adidas US Sales dataset.

The dashboard analyzes total sales, operating profit, units sold, and pricing across regions, states, products, and retailers for the period 2020–2021.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total sales and operating profit
- Track units sold and average price per unit
- Compare sales performance across U.S. states
- Analyze sales by region (West, Northeast, Southeast, South, Midwest)
- Compare sales by product category
- Compare sales performance across retailers

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### Sales by Month
Monthly sales trend shows fluctuations across the year, with peaks in July ($95M) and August ($92M).

### Sales by State
A geographic view highlighting the highest-performing states by total sales.

### Sales by Region
The West region generates the highest share of sales (30%), followed by Northeast (20.7%) and Southeast (18.13%).

### Sales by Product
Men's Street Footwear and Women's Apparel are the top-performing product categories.

### Sales by Retailer
West Gear and Foot Locker are the top revenue-generating retailers.
